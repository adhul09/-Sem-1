# AI/LLM Awareness

## a. What is AI and modern applications?

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** is a technology that enables computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing images, making predictions and solving problems.

Some common applications of AI include:

- Voice assistants and chatbots
- Recommendation systems on YouTube, Netflix, etc.
- Image and face recognition
- Fraud detection in banking
- Self-driving and driver-assistance systems
- Medical image analysis
- Generative AI for creating text, images, audio and code

AI systems can use large amounts of data to identify patterns and make predictions or generate outputs.

## b. What are LLMs?

**LLM (Large Language Model)** is a type of AI model designed to understand and generate human-like text.

LLMs are trained on very large amounts of text data. They learn patterns in language and can use those patterns to generate responses based on a given input.

Examples of tasks LLMs can perform include:

- Answering questions
- Summarizing text
- Translating languages
- Generating and explaining code
- Writing or modifying text
- Having conversations with users

An LLM does not think exactly like a human. It generates responses by predicting suitable sequences of text based on patterns learned during training.  

Examples of applications that use LLMs include ChatGPT and Claude.  
Applications can use LLMs through APIs.

```text
User input
    ↓
Text is processed into smaller units
    ↓
LLM analyzes the input and its context
    ↓
The model predicts suitable next tokens
    ↓
Tokens are generated one after another
    ↓
Final response
```
LLMs generally do not process text exactly as humans see it. Text is divided into smaller units called tokens.

A token can be a complete word, part of a word, punctuation mark or another piece of text.

For example, a sentence such as:  
'I am learning JavaScript.'  
is converted into tokens before being processed by the model.
The exact way text is divided into tokens depends on the tokenizer being used.  


## c. What AI can and cannot do

AI can perform many tasks quickly and efficiently, especially tasks involving large amounts of data and recognizable patterns.

**AI can:**

- Analyze and process large amounts of information
- Recognize patterns
- Generate text, images, audio and code
- Automate repetitive tasks
- Assist humans in solving problems and making decisions

**AI cannot always:**

- Provide completely accurate information
- Understand the real world exactly like a human
- Know whether its generated information is actually true
- Make reliable decisions in every situation
- Replace human judgment in situations requiring responsibility, context or real-world understanding

AI can sometimes produce incorrect or misleading information while presenting it confidently. Therefore, important information should be verified.

## d. Risks of over-reliance

Over-reliance on AI means depending on AI too much without understanding or checking its output.

Some risks include:

- Learning less if AI is used instead of thinking and solving problems independently
- Accepting incorrect information without verification
- Becoming dependent on AI for simple tasks
- Security and privacy risks when sensitive information is shared
- Difficulty identifying mistakes in AI-generated code or content
- Losing important problem-solving and critical-thinking skills

AI should therefore be used as a **tool to assist humans**, rather than blindly depending on it.
